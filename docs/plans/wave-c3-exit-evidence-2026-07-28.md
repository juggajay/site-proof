diverge. All three are **accepted**; all three were disclosed by the PR that made them
rather than found by a reviewer. Recorded here so a later reader does not re-litigate them
or mistake them for drift.

## What C3 actually delivers at HEAD

A tenth map toolbar toggle that recolours drawn lots to the **same three-valued verdict the
lot page shows** — proven identical on two routes, one of which was **returning a different
answer until this wave fixed it** — plus a magenta pin wherever a human explicitly said the
sample was taken, with its provenance and its accuracy visible in the popup. Nothing is
derived, nothing is defaulted, nothing is cached offline, nothing verifies a test, nothing
switches a project into `block`. A test with a chainage string and no coordinate is
**counted and not drawn**, and two mutation tests exist solely to make sure it stays that
way.

The wave's other product is a **negative** one, and it is the more valuable: C3.0 proved the
program's "LIMS tabulated ingestion" premise described a flow that does not exist, so the
parser, the import kind and the export path were **not built** — the second confirmed
instance of the unread-grade-A-appendix-row pattern after R44.

The six open items are what stands between that and "a QM on a pilot project trusts the
overlay": Jay's live round-trip and his phone (one session closes both), one missing test,
and four write-backs across three items — two of them into files that live outside this
repo, which is exactly why those are the ones most likely to rot.
