## What Wave E actually delivers at HEAD

A shared predicate that finally matches the status production actually writes, so an
internal alert for an overdue external release **can fire for the first time** — the wave's
founding finding was that it never had, and AT-98 proves it by running the old query against
a row the real route created and getting zero. On top of that, an hourly job that chases the

the manual route and the job share, with `replyTo` pointing at the human who actually asked
— which it did not before, because the chase email had been telling the superintendent the
request came from the superintendent's own address.

blank, whitespace and separator-only all resolve to zero projects, proven by eleven tests
and by injecting the exact `?.split(',')` regression E.0 predicted. The public link surface
got **smaller**, not larger: three disclosures left it before any of this shipped, and the
first negative assertion on a public payload in the repo now holds the line. The wave adds
no public route, no new model, no capability that spans more than one hold point, and one
index.

storage design blind to exactly the tokens a reminder mints. Deferring it is `[E-g]`'s
largest single act of laziness and the most valuable thing in the wave.
